Paintings 2018–2026
Yiğit Özen

One hundred and fifty works: the 35 of Paintings since 2019 and the 115 that had not been catalogued. They are ordered by what they are made on rather than by when — 32 on canvas, then 109 on paper, carton and print, then 9 that are objects — and within each the catalogued work leads. Four to a page, the plate falling to one side and then the other down the page, and the page facing it keeping the same beat rather than answering it.
